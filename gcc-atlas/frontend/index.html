📖 Sourcing Methodology & Governance

GCC Atlas compiles public intelligence from institutional authorities: state investment facilitation agencies (TS-iPASS, Karnataka KITS, Invest UP, Guidance Tamil Nadu), statutory filings (SEC 10-K, MCA), official corporate newsrooms, and respected industry research bodies (NASSCOM, ANAROCK, Michael Page).

Data Integrity Commitment: We adhere to a strict zero-fabrication standard. If specific figures (such as unverified establishment years or confidential lease terms) cannot be corroborated through primary public evidence, they are transparently displayed as "Data unavailable".
